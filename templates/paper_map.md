Attach the exact screenshot/crop of the representative method/system/algorithm/architecture figure from the PDF/page using `--figure-file` or `--figure-url`. Do not redraw or generate a substitute diagram. Do not use Mermaid/ASCII/SVG schematics in the main figure slot. Do not use experiment/result plots unless no method figure exists. Do not write provenance text like “Exact crop of …” or raw PDF-extracted broken caption text in HTML. The renderer places this section directly under the image and removes this heading from the body.

Open the cropped image and write each label from what is literally drawn — not from the caption or generic priors. Never write generic reading advice such as “follow the arrows” or “read left to right”.
