# Korean Support Checklist

- [ ] Korean prompt receives Korean prose.
- [ ] LaTeX notation remains unchanged.
- [ ] Technical terms are explained rather than merely translated.
- [ ] The response reconnects to the original paper location.
- [ ] Common research terms stay in English when natural: `training objective`, `objective function`, `loss`, `gradient`, `generator`, `distribution`, `pushforward`, `drift field`, `inference`, `sample`, `parameter`.
- [ ] Awkward literal translations of common research vocabulary are avoided; prefer the standard English term when that is how researchers read it.
