# Figure Explanation Checklist

- [ ] Paper map lists important figures, diagrams, tables, or states that none are present.
- [ ] Method/system/algorithm figures are explained before the method is treated as understood.
- [ ] Each figure explanation names components, flow or sequence, what to observe, and the conclusion.
- [ ] Figure explanation links back to supporting equations, assumptions, algorithms, or claims.
- [ ] Visual confusion is repaired with a minimal example when needed.
