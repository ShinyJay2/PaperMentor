# Code of Conduct

PaperMentor is a learning-focused open-source project. We welcome contributions from researchers, students, engineers, educators, and readers who want papers to become easier to reconstruct.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and precise.
- Critique ideas and outputs, not people.
- Prefer reproducible examples over vague criticism.
- Help contributors improve equations, derivations, dependencies, and explanations.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Spam or promotional content unrelated to paper understanding.
- Deliberately misleading mathematical explanations.
- Sharing private or copyrighted paper content when you do not have the right to do so.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns through the repository security/contact channel or by opening a private maintainer conversation where available. Maintainers may remove content, close issues, or block participants to protect the project community.
